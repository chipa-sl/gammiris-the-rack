Stock awareness & mindful household habits

Small details.
Everyday difference.

Two purposes, side by side: know what’s running low and stay aware of how your household uses salt and sugar.

01

Monitor ingredient stock

Keep an eye on what’s left, know when to refill, and plan what to buy.

  • Track weight-based stock estimates for seven ingredients.
  • See low-stock alerts at your chosen minimum levels.
  • Plan shopping and keep refill updates current.
Explore stock monitoring →
02

Support mindful household habits

Build household-use awareness with salt and sugar insights and goals you choose.

  • Track estimated amounts of salt and sugar removed from household containers.
  • Review household usage trends over time.
  • Set, adjust, or pause voluntary household goals.
  • Use optional goal locking with an authorized override.
Explore household insights →

Household usage estimates describe amounts removed from shared containers, not an individual’s intake.
