A closer look at the app

Stock awareness.
Mindful household habits.

Four interface previews show how ingredient organization and voluntary household goals come together.

Illustrative interface designs with example data, not live readings. Health-related labels and values shown in these mockups are not personal intake measurements, medical advice, or recommended limits. Goals are voluntary household settings.

01 · Ingredient awareness

Monitor ingredient stock

Keep an eye on what’s left, know when to refill, and plan what to buy. Track weight-based stock estimates for seven ingredients, review low-stock alerts, and keep shopping lists and refill updates current.

02 · Household-use awareness

Support mindful household habits

Review estimated amounts of salt and sugar removed from household containers and usage trends over time. Set, adjust, or pause voluntary household goals, with optional goal locking and an authorized override.
