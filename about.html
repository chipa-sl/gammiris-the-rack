A little about us

Rooted in flavor.
Made for everyday.

Gammiris is the brand behind The Rack—a smart kitchen organizer concept inspired by a simple thought: the everyday ingredients you reach for should be easy to organize and easier to keep track of.

From the warmth of spices to the small rituals of preparing a meal, a kitchen is full of details that matter. Our approach is to make one of those details—spice storage—feel a little more thoughtful.

Monitor ingredient stock

Keep everyday essentials organized and make shopping and refills easier to plan.

  • Track weight-based stock estimates for seven ingredients.
  • See low-stock alerts at your chosen minimum levels.
  • Plan shopping and keep refill updates current.
⌂

Support mindful household habits

Make household-use awareness part of everyday routines, with voluntary goals under your household’s control.

  • Track estimated amounts of salt and sugar removed from household containers.
  • Review household usage trends over time.
  • Set, adjust, or pause voluntary household goals.
  • Use optional goal locking with an authorized override.

Usage trends reflect estimated amounts removed from household containers; they do not measure an individual’s intake.
